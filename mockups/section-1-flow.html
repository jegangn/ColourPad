Notes for this section

  • Home: Two huge tiles fill the screen — minimal choice for a 5yo. Gallery is a small pill below, gear (parent area) is small in the corner.
  • Picker: Difficulty row appears only for Connect-the-Dots. Colouring goes straight to the grid. Grid scrolls horizontally if more than ~12 fit on screen.
  • Done: After he finishes, the picture is shown briefly with the voice naming it. He can save manually (⭐), try another, or go home. Nothing saves automatically.
  • Categories filter: With 200 pictures, he'll need a way to find vehicles. Top of the picker has tappable category chips: Vehicles, Animals, Dinosaurs, Food, etc. Not shown above for clarity.
