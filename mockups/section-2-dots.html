How the drag-a-line mechanic works

  • Start: Dot 1 has a soft pulsing ring as a hint. Pressing any other dot does nothing — only dot 1 starts the line.
  • Drag detection: As his finger moves, we track its position. When it gets within ~30px of the next-in-sequence dot, that dot "snaps" — line locks in, dot turns green, chime plays, the next dot starts pulsing.
  • Wrong direction: If he drags near a wrong dot (e.g., dot 7 when on 5→6), nothing happens. No buzzer, no penalty. The line just keeps trailing his finger.
  • Lift finger mid-puzzle: Line freezes at last completed dot. He can resume by pressing that dot again. (Important — 5yos lift their fingers a lot.)
  • Completion: Last dot snapped → trail fades into the full coloured picture, voice says the name. Done buttons slide up from the bottom.
  • Faint outline: Background outline (dashed) is shown at very low opacity as a nudge. Can be toggled off in parent settings if too easy.
